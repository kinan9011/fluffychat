<b>Your communication should be yours.</b>

Odango is an open-source Matrix messenger with a familiar, Telegram-style interface. It is end-to-end encrypted, decentralized, and easy to use.

<b>Secure</b>

Odango supports Matrix end-to-end encryption, encrypted message backups and device verification.

<b>Decentral</b>

Based on the open Matrix protocol, Odango works with any Matrix server and is compatible with other Matrix clients like Element, Nheko, Cinny or NeoChat. Learn more at matrix.org.

<b>A fork of FluffyChat</b>

Odango is a fork of FluffyChat and keeps its AGPL-3.0 licence and upstream attribution. Part of the support you give Odango goes on to support the FluffyChat source it is built on.
